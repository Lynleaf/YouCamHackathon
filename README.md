# YouCam

### Your AI-powered personal color stylist

**YouCam** uses AI to analyze your **skin, eyes, and hair** to determine your personal color season and create a personalized color palette.

📱 **[Download the Android APK](https://github.com/Lynleaf/YouCamHackathon/releases/latest)**

---

## ✨ What is YouCam?

Choosing colors that complement your appearance can be surprisingly difficult.

YouCam makes personal color analysis accessible in seconds. Simply upload a photo, and YouCam analyzes your coloring to determine your **12-season color palette** and recommend colors that work best for you.

Instead of relying on expensive consultations or complicated color charts, YouCam puts personalized color analysis directly on your phone.

---

## 🎨 Features

### 📸 AI Color Analysis

Upload a photo and let AI analyze your:

* 🧑 Skin tone
* 👁️ Eye color
* 💇 Hair color

### 🌈 Personalized Color Season

YouCam uses the analysis to determine your personal color season from the **12-season color system**:

* Spring
* Summer
* Autumn
* Winter
* 3 subclassifications dependent on dominant season (light, bright, true, etc.)

### 🎨 Your Personal Palette

Once your season is determined, YouCam generates a personalized palette.

Each color is represented with its corresponding color value, making the recommendations easy to understand and use.
